  <h1 id="meaning-title">Meaning and Culture</h1>
  <p>Semiotics broadens the analysis by treating communication as a process of signification. Messages do not only travel; they become meaningful through symbols, conventions, and cultural frames.</p>
  <p>This lens helps explain why the same phrase, image, or AI-generated response may be interpreted differently across communities. It also reveals how digital interfaces encode assumptions about users and contexts.</p>
  <p>Use this page to analyze how a communication phenomenon creates meaning, not just how it distributes information.</p>
