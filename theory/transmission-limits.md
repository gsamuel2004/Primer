  <h1 id="transmission-title">The Transmission View and Its Limits</h1>
  <p>The Shannon-Weaver model clarifies how messages move through channels, where noise interrupts transmission, and why signal quality matters. It is especially useful when communication problems involve technical systems, platform constraints, or data loss.</p>
  <p>At the same time, a humanist critique asks what the model leaves out: social context, lived experience, ambiguity, and values. A mathematically successful transmission can still fail as human communication.</p>
  <p>Use this section to build your own case studies that compare efficiency of transmission with quality of understanding.</p>
