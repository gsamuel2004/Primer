  <h1 id="intention-title">Interpretation and Intention</h1>
  <p>Umberto Eco distinguishes among intention of the author, text, and reader. This triad remains powerful for analyzing ambiguity and interpretive limits.</p>
  <p>For AI-mediated communication, an additional layer can be proposed: <em>intentio algorithmi</em>. This names the operational tendency of algorithmic systems—the patterns encouraged by objectives, training data, and ranking logic—even when no human author directly intended a specific output.</p>
  <p>Use this page to test where classical intent frameworks hold and where algorithmic mediation requires new vocabulary.</p>
