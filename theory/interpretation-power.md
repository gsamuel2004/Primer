  <h1 id="power-title">Interpretation and Power</h1>
  <p>Stuart Hall's encoding/decoding model shows that meaning is negotiated, not guaranteed. Producers encode preferred meanings, but audiences decode from different social positions.</p>
  <p>This makes power visible: institutions, platforms, and dominant groups often shape which interpretations are considered legitimate. In AI contexts, this can include who designs models, what data is selected, and whose language is normalized.</p>
  <p>Use this page to map dominant, negotiated, and oppositional readings of one communication problem.</p>
