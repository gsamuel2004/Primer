  <h1 id="home-overview">Communication Theory for Everyday Communication Problems</h1>
  <p class="lead">This project introduces major schools of communication theory for readers who are not communication specialists but still face communication challenges in daily life and work.</p>
  <p>This website is a structured guide for understanding how communication theory can make the commonplace visible. It connects foundational ideas to 21st-century issues, especially those shaped by artificial intelligence and algorithmic systems.</p>
